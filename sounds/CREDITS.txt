Game sound effects for data-war / VX-7 project
All files are free to use in commercial games (CC0 / public domain).

Sources:
- shot0, shot1, shot2, en: Trimmed from The Free Firearm Sound Library (CC0) via OpenGameArt
  - shot0: AR-15 (VX-7 Carbine style)
  - shot1: Carl Gustav M45 SMG (Needle SMG)
  - shot2: Savage 10 .300 Blackout (Rail-9 Marksman style)
  - en: AK-47 (enemy fire)
- rl: Gun reload, lock or click sound (CC0) from OpenGameArt
- hit, hurt: From 100 CC0 SFX pack (rubberduck / OpenGameArt)
- kill: chop.ogg from Kenney RPG Audio (CC0)
- step: footstep01.ogg from Kenney RPG Audio (CC0)

Files are trimmed to start near the action, mono 44.1kHz OGG, under 15KB each.
Drop them into your sounds folder (names match the slots). Existing synthesized sounds are replaced only for files you provide.
