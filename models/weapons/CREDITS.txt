3D Gun Models for game (GLB format)
All files are free to use in commercial games (CC0 / public domain). No attribution required.

Source: Flat Guns West / Flat Guns East from OpenGameArt (via fps-asset-kit), CC0.

Suggested mapping for your weapons:
- Rifle_Assault_West.glb or Rifle_Assault_East.glb → VX-7 Carbine
- SMG_Full_West.glb or SMG_Compact_West.glb → Needle SMG
- Sniper_Rifle_West.glb or Sniper_Rifle_East.glb → Rail-9 Marksman
- Pistol variants → sidearms
- Shotgun variants → additional weapons

West and East are two style variants (slightly different shapes/materials).

Files are already GLB (ready for most engines: Three.js, Unity, Godot, Unreal, etc.).
Low-poly, game-ready, small file sizes.

Drop into your models/weapons folder and load by name.
